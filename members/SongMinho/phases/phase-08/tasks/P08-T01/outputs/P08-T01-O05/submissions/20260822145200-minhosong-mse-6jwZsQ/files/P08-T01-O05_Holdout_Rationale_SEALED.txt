Why these four
1. P5 electrical-unseen
All four lie on the new 1-nm interstitial grid and were not part of the P5 2-nm electrical campaign.
This prevents known P5 electrical outcomes from contaminating the independent P9 test.

2. Not direct P7 local-slope points
P7 direct local-slope calculations used the nominal and existing P5 neighbors such as 33/67, 35/67, 37/67, 35/65 and 35/63.
The four Holdouts were not direct finite-difference input points.

3. Not P6 preflight cases
The P6 visual preflight cases were 35/67, 33/67, 37/67, 35/65 and 35/69.
The Holdouts therefore were not even pre-run geometry/mesh demonstration cases.

4. Preserve the two most important P8 centerlines
No Holdout lies on B1=35 or B2=67.
Therefore the complete nominal centerlines remain available to P08-T02-O03 OAT/sensitivity analysis.

5. Preserve the outer grid boundary
No Holdout lies on B1=33/37 or B2=63/69.
P8 keeps all candidate-grid edges for learning the first 2D tolerance boundary.

6. Four-quadrant geometry coverage
The set spans:
- low B1 / low B2
- low B1 / high B2
- high B1 / low B2
- high B1 / high B2
around the nominal neighborhood.

7. Near/far distance diversity
The set includes Euclidean distances of about 1.41 nm and 3.16 nm from 35/67.
It is therefore not only a tight near-nominal cluster.

8. Why four instead of two or three
Issue #8 permits 2–4 Holdouts.
Four leaves 31/35 = 88.6% of the candidate grid active while maximizing the allowed number of independent P9 validation cases.
Because these four are interior and off-centerline, their P8 information cost is low compared with withholding edge or centerline points.

9. Independence rule after sealing
These four coordinates must be excluded from:
- P08-T02 2D electrical execution and Window construction,
- P08-T03 3D representative selection,
- P08-T04 3D calibration.
P08-T04 may only record the final calibrated Window's prediction for them.
Their first actual validation execution is P09-T02 1T1C.

10. No outcome-based replacement
Once P8 electrical results begin, these coordinates must not be swapped because another point looks more interesting or produces a more convenient expected result.
