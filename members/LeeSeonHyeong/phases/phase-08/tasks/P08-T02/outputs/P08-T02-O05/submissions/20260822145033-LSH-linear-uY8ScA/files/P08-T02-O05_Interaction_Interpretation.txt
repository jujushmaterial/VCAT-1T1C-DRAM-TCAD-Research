P08-T02-O05 — Interaction interpretation

Primary evidence: preregistered active 31-point P8 grid only.

Observed pattern:
- At nominal centerlines, all tested Xbnd1 and Xbnd2 points remain Device PASS.
- The adverse Xbnd1 effect is stronger at low Xbnd2.
- The beneficial Xbnd2 effect is stronger at high Xbnd1.
- This conditional-slope dependence produces a lower-right interaction strip:
  36/63, 37/63, 37/64 = MARGINAL.
- All three MARGINAL points are limited by DIBL.
- No Device FAIL is observed inside the official active 31-point domain.

Interpretation boundary:
This supports an Xbnd1–Xbnd2 interaction within the tested L-H-L gate-segmentation
geometry domain. It does not establish a general process-variation model or a
3D/DRAM tolerance boundary.
